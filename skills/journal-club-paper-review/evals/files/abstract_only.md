Cooperative Adaptive Cruise Control Eliminates Freeway Stop-and-Go Waves: A Comprehensive
Simulation Study. A. Rivera, B. Chen, C. Okafor. Journal of Synthetic Transportation
Studies, 2026.

Abstract. In today's rapidly evolving transportation landscape, cooperative adaptive cruise
control (CACC) plays a pivotal role in seamless traffic operations. This paper delves into
the effect of CACC on freeway congestion. We propose a novel CACC controller and evaluate
it in SUMO on a freeway segment with an on-ramp. Results show that CACC reduces average
delay by 32% and eliminates stop-and-go waves. The results are validated with real-world
field data. The findings provide comprehensive, robust and actionable insights for
agencies.
