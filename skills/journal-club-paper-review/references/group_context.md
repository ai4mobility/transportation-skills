# Group context

Used only for "Are there useful connections to our group's research?" [S2]. Each group
edits this file. If the presenter names specific projects, use those instead. If neither
fits the paper, ask the presenter rather than guess.

Default (the instructor's group at USF):
- Traffic flow theory.
- Vehicle technologies (ADAS, lane keeping assist, electric vehicles) and their impact on
  traffic efficiency and safety.
- AI in mobility: AI dash cameras for traffic monitoring, road hazard detection, AI-based
  safety alert systems, and AI-enabled traffic operations.
