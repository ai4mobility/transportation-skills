# Practice: what experienced staff do that the SOP does not say (NOT the D7 SOP)

Practice comes from people, not documents: how experienced signal staff fill the SOP's gaps,
the order they really work in, and the mistakes they see new staff make. Collect it by
interviewing someone who has worked a storm (see `docs/INTERVIEW_THE_EXPERT.md` in the ai4mobility/transportation-skills repository).

Rules for this file:

- Write down only what a named person said. Record their role and the date.
- If the person is playing a role (as in the Nov 17 class demo, where the instructor plays a
  district signal engineer), add "role-played" to **Said by**. Those entries are class
  examples, not D7 practice.
- One entry per idea, in their words where you can.
- An entry is a **suggestion**, never a rule: cite it by its id (P1, P2, ...) in the
  "Suggestion" format of `assets/action_card.md`, and set **Person decides: yes**.
- If an entry contradicts the SOP, keep both and flag the conflict. Do not pick a winner.
- Re-check entries every season. Practice changes faster than the SOP.

## Entry format

```
## P<n>
- **Practice:** <what they do, and why, in one or two sentences>
- **Said by:** <role, not a name>, <date>
- **Use when:** <the situation it applies to>
- **Who decides:** <role>
```
