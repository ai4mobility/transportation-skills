# What the SOP does not say (v1.5, June 2025)

Read this before writing cards. When an action depends on one of these, say so
in the card and name who to ask. Do not fill the gap yourself.

1. **Appendix B, Hurricane Response Zone Maps (PDF p. 24), is blank.** Three Hours
   Prior 2.1 says arterial RTMC personnel "reorder, prioritize, and distribute all
   hurricane response zone maps", but the maps are not in the document.
   Ask: arterial RTMC.
2. **Appendix C, Responsibility Matrix (PDF p. 25), is blank.** Many steps name
   no role ("Track generator pickups and redeployments"). Write
   "not stated (Appendix C is blank)" in **Who**.
3. **No list of Priority #1 intersections.** Three Hours Prior 2.2 refers to them.
   TMC personnel decide generator priorities.
4. **Refueling after the first one.** Immediately Post Storm 3.2 says "based on
   fueling needs". Only the first refuel (six hours) is a rule.
5. **$5,000: "or greater" or "exceeds"?** Funding 0-1 Month 1 says "$5000 or
   greater" and 0-1 Week 6.1.1 says "=> $5,000", but 0-1 Month 2.1 says "if the total
   cost exceeds $5,000". A group totaling exactly $5,000 goes to the DDIR team.
6. **What counts as a 1/4-mile group.** The SOP gives the distance, not the method
   (from the first repair? end to end?). The DDIR team decides (TWO 4).
7. **0-1 Week 6.2.1 points to the "48 Hours Prior to Storm Event" section.** The
   section's actual title is "48 Hours Prior to Storm".
8. **Damage assessment pages and Appendix A (Field Checklist) are not in this
   skill.** Damage codes come from the agency's form (see the form demo).
9. **The weights are a reading of the verbs** (must / standard / consider), not an
   FDOT classification. A person settles borderline cases.
10. **Typos are quoted as written.** One Week Prior 7.1 says "City of Tamp".
