# Field words → the SOP's words

People in the field rarely use the SOP's terms. "Top off the backup power units" means
**refueling** the **generators**; "the lights are blinking" means a signal **in flash**.
Before you look for clauses, map the request's words to the SOP's words with this table,
then search `sop_text.md` for the SOP's words. Keep the user's words in your answer only
where they are clearer; cite the SOP in its own words.

**Source and limits.** Written for class on Nov 14, 2026 from the SOP's own vocabulary
(PDF pages 4-11) and common usage in Florida signal operations, for the RAG glossary test
(48 entries). It was not written with D7 staff. A real glossary is built with the
operators, crews and district staff who use both vocabularies: add their words at the bottom,
with who said it and when.
